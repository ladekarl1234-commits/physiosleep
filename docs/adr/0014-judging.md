# ADR 0014: Independent simulated judging under frozen rubrics

Date: 2026-09-24

Status: **Accepted**

## Context

The project must preserve reproducibility and distinguish development, confirmation and provisional downstream work. See the [current report](../REPORT.md) for measured evidence.

## Decision

Use separate specialist and data/ML reviewer agents; freeze scoring criteria, fix actual findings and rescore identically.

## Alternatives and consequences

Agent grades are not official competition or clinical approval. Wording cannot earn missing experimental evidence points.

## Verification and revisit condition

Preserved initial/final rubric records and change ledger. Revisit only through an explicit versioned decision; preserve prior evidence and confirmation exposure history.
