# ADR 0003: Participant-disjoint development and two audits

Date: 2026-09-24

Status: **Accepted**

## Context

The project must preserve reproducibility and distinguish development, confirmation and provisional downstream work. See the [current report](../REPORT.md) for measured evidence.

## Decision

Freeze D60/A20/B20 and five participant folds. All nights and learned artifacts follow membership.

## Alternatives and consequences

Random epoch splits leak person/night information. Audits on a shared account remain procedural.

## Verification and revisit condition

Split and audit-access tests; separate truth and signal-only inference. Revisit only through an explicit versioned decision; preserve prior evidence and confirmation exposure history.
