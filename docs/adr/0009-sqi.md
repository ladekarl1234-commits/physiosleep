# ADR 0009: Provisional duration/continuity index

Date: 2026-09-24

Status: **Provisional; targets unmet**

## Context

The project must preserve reproducibility and distinguish development, confirmation and provisional downstream work. See the [current report](../REPORT.md) for measured evidence.

## Decision

Keep a two-component experimental formula, conservative coverage and independently defined eligibility. TIB and attempt-to-sleep anchors cannot be invented.

## Alternatives and consequences

Reference-formula agreement is not clinical validity. All tested sensors fail joint score/component tolerances; minimum sensors remain unknown.

## Verification and revisit condition

Summary edge cases, constant predictors and participant-balanced adverse results. Revisit only through an explicit versioned decision; preserve prior evidence and confirmation exposure history.
