# ADR 0011: Measurement-first hardware planning

Date: 2026-09-24

Status: **Provisional desk design**

## Context

The project must preserve reproducibility and distinguish development, confirmation and provisional downstream work. See the [current report](../REPORT.md) for measured evidence.

## Decision

Define montage, calibration, timing, packets and budgets before hardware selection or model transfer. Separate channels from electrode contacts.

## Alternatives and consequences

Fpz−Cz cannot become a forehead-only montage; EOG geometry unresolved. Synthetic tests are not physical safety or accuracy evidence.

## Verification and revisit condition

Generated engineering checks; physical B01–B10 and paired device study pending. Revisit only through an explicit versioned decision; preserve prior evidence and confirmation exposure history.
